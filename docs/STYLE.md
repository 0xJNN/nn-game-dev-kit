# Pilot style direction — awaiting human art approval

- Active direction: first-person Unreal/Linux research interior; no new Godot/Mac visual work.
- Architectural rhythm, separate equipment bays, service channels, and practical light panels.
- Dark navy environment; teal primary interactions; warm amber inactive/reference elements.
- Readable silhouettes, simple geometry, meter-based dimensions, restrained material roughness.
- Review at the first-person gameplay camera and real player height, not only isolated renders.
- Minimal typography; no third-party game names, logos, or copied interfaces.
- The current look is a technical greybox/vertical slice, not final Novanode branding.
- Prefer repeatable geometry and reusable materials over generating decorative variations.
- Proposed art changes need a small comparison sheet, an in-game capture, and human approval.
